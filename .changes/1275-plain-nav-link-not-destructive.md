### Fixed

- **`@reticlehq/browser` — a plain navigation link is no longer refused as a destructive action.** The guard reads a control's `href` along with its text, which is right for a button whose label is an icon: its `formAction` is the only place it says what it does. On an anchor the href is an ADDRESS, and addresses carry the pattern's words, so `Orders & invoices` pointing at `/billing/payment` was blocked for the `payment` in the URL, and the way past a block is `confirmDangerous: true` passed reflexively. A same-origin `<a href>` with no inline handler and no form around it is a GET that changes nothing, so it is now classified on the act it performs rather than on its address. A link with an `onclick`, or one inside a form, keeps its block. Closes [#1275](https://github.com/reticlehq/reticle/issues/1275).
